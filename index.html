How Texas Rated works

Simple enough for consumers, useful enough for business owners.

1

Public data snapshot

We collect public ratings, review counts and company information and date every refresh.

2

Texas Rated Score

Public reputation is adjusted for review depth so a perfect score on a handful of reviews does not automatically lead the market.

3

Company profiles

Every tracked company gets a profile page. Owners can claim and verify theirs to add richer, confirmed information.

4

Owner tools

Claimed businesses can manage their profile, receive leads and messages, track rank, and install a live Texas Rated emblem.
