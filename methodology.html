How the Texas Rated Score works

The Texas Rated Score is designed to combine public reputation, recent momentum and verified company information into a comparable 100-point score within each local service market.

55% weight
Reputation

Public rating strength, review volume, review quality, longevity and other defensible reputation signals.

20% weight
Momentum

Recent review growth and changes relative to comparable companies in the same location and category.

15% weight
Verified Profile Data

Owner verification, service coverage, business details and other information that can be verified rather than inferred.

10% weight
Response Performance

As Texas Rated generates first-party inquiries, response rate and response speed can provide a measured service signal.

Non-negotiable
Rankings aren't sold

Advertising, lead participation, badge installation and outbound links do not purchase a higher Texas Rated rank.

Owner participation
Better data

Completing a verified profile can improve the verified-data component because Texas Rated has more reliable business information to score.
